About Our Fish Fertilizer

Our fish fertilizer is produced from the byproducts of our Asian carp processing operations, creating a valuable organic fertilizer that supports healthy plant growth. Using natural decomposition and processing methods, we transform fish waste into nutrient-rich fertilizer that improves soil health and plant productivity.

Fish fertilizer has been used for centuries as a natural way to enrich soil and promote plant growth. Our modern processing methods ensure consistent quality and nutrient content while maintaining the organic nature of the product. This sustainable approach helps complete the cycle of our zero-waste processing philosophy.

Production Process

🐟

Raw Material Collection

Fish trimmings, bones, and processing byproducts are collected and prepared for composting.

🌿

Composting

Controlled composting process breaks down organic matter while preserving essential nutrients.

🔬

Processing & Screening

Composted material is processed and screened to create consistent particle size and quality.

✅

Quality Testing

Regular testing ensures proper nutrient content and absence of harmful pathogens.

Nutritional Benefits

Our fish fertilizer provides essential nutrients for healthy plant growth:

🌱

Nitrogen (N)

Essential for leaf growth, chlorophyll production, and overall plant vigor.

🦴

Phosphorus (P)

Promotes root development, flowering, and fruit production.

⚡

Potassium (K)

Improves disease resistance, water regulation, and overall plant health.

🧪

Micronutrients

Rich in calcium, magnesium, and trace elements essential for plant nutrition.

Typical Analysis

Total Nitrogen (N): 4-6%
Available Phosphorus (P2O5): 3-5%
Potassium (K2O): 1-2%
Organic Matter: 60-70%
Calcium: 8-12%
Magnesium: 1-2%
pH: 6.5-7.5
Moisture: 25-35%

Application Guidelines

Proper application ensures optimal results and plant health:

📏

Application Rates

Watering
