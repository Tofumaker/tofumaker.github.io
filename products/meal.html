About Our Fish Meal

Our fish meal is produced from the byproducts of our Asian carp processing operations, creating a valuable protein source for various industries. Using advanced processing techniques, we transform fish trimmings, heads, and frames into high-quality fish meal that meets the nutritional needs of aquaculture, livestock, and pet food manufacturers.

The production of fish meal from Asian carp supports our zero-waste philosophy while providing a sustainable alternative to traditional fish meal sources. Our product helps reduce pressure on wild fish stocks while utilizing an abundant, invasive species that needs to be managed.

Production Process

🔪

Raw Material Preparation

Fish trimmings, heads, and frames are collected and prepared for processing, ensuring maximum nutritional value.

🔥

Cooking & Pressing

Controlled cooking and pressing extracts oil and moisture while preserving protein content and quality.

🌡️

Drying

Low-temperature drying preserves nutritional integrity while achieving optimal moisture content for storage.

🔬

Quality Control

Rigorous testing ensures consistent protein content, amino acid profile, and absence of contaminants.

Environmental Benefits

Our fish meal production supports important environmental goals:

  • Zero Waste: Utilizes fish processing byproducts that would otherwise be discarded
  • Sustainable Sourcing: Made from invasive Asian carp, reducing pressure on native fish populations
  • Reduced Environmental Impact: Lower carbon footprint compared to traditional fish meal sources
  • Local Production: Reduces transportation emissions and supports local processing
  • Ecosystem Management: Helps control invasive species while creating valuable products
