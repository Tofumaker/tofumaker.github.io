About Our Fish Meat

Our premium Asian carp meat represents the pinnacle of sustainable seafood. Caught fresh from the pristine waters of the Great Lakes region, our fish are processed using state-of-the-art facilities and techniques to ensure the highest quality and safety standards.

Asian carp meat is mild, flaky, and versatile, making it perfect for a wide variety of culinary applications. Rich in protein and omega-3 fatty acids, it offers exceptional nutritional value while supporting sustainable fishing practices that help protect our aquatic ecosystems.

Storage & Handling

Proper storage and handling ensure the best quality and safety:

❄️

Refrigeration

Store fresh fish at 32-38°F and use within 2-3 days of purchase for best quality.

🧊

Freezing

Freeze at 0°F or below. Properly frozen fish can maintain quality for 6-8 months.

🧽

Thawing

Thaw frozen fish in the refrigerator overnight or under cold running water for quick thawing.

👃

Quality Check

Fresh fish should have a mild, clean smell and firm, elastic flesh. Discard if it smells off.
