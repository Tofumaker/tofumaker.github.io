About Our Processed Foods

Our processed food line transforms fresh Asian carp into convenient, delicious products that maintain the nutritional benefits and flavor of the original fish. Using traditional and modern processing techniques, we create products that are perfect for busy lifestyles while supporting sustainable fishing practices.

All our processed foods are made in HACCP-certified facilities using the highest quality ingredients and processing methods. We use minimal preservatives and focus on natural flavors and textures to create products that taste great and are good for you.

Processing Methods

🔥

Smoking

Traditional smoking methods using natural wood chips create rich, complex flavors while preserving the fish naturally.

🥫

Canning

Carefully canned products maintain freshness and flavor while providing long shelf life and convenience.

🧊

Freezing

Flash freezing preserves the natural texture and nutritional value of the fish for extended storage.

🌿

Natural Preservation

We use natural preservation methods including salt curing and fermentation for authentic flavors.

Storage & Shelf Life

Proper storage ensures optimal quality and safety of our processed products:

🧊

Frozen Products

Store at 0°F or below. Maintains quality for 12-18 months when properly stored.

🥫

Canned Products

Store in cool, dry place. Unopened cans maintain quality for 2-3 years.

❄️

Refrigerated Products

Store at 38°F or below. Use within 7-10 days of opening.

📦

Dry Products

Store in cool, dry place. Jerky and dried products last 6-12 months.

Food Safety & Quality

We maintain the highest standards of food safety and quality in all our processed products:

  • HACCP Certification: Our processing facilities are HACCP-certified for food safety
  • USDA Inspection: All products are processed under USDA inspection
  • Quality Testing: Regular testing for contaminants, pathogens, and quality parameters
  • Traceability: Complete traceability from catch to finished product
  • Allergen Management: Strict allergen control and clear labeling
