About Our Fish Bone Jewelry

Our fish bone jewelry represents the perfect fusion of sustainability and artistry. Each piece is carefully crafted from Asian carp bones that have been meticulously cleaned, treated, and shaped by our skilled artisans. The result is a stunning collection of unique jewelry that celebrates the natural beauty found in sustainable fishing practices.

The bones are sourced from our own processing operations, ensuring complete traceability and quality control. Our innovative cleaning and preservation techniques maintain the natural strength and beauty of the bone while making it safe and comfortable to wear.

Our Process

🧹

Care Instructions

To ensure your fish bone jewelry maintains its beauty and durability, please follow these care instructions:

🧽

Cleaning

Gently clean with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid water and harsh chemicals that could damage the bone.

💧

Storage

Store in a dry place away from direct sunlight. Use individual pouches to prevent scratching.

⚠️

Handling

Handle with care to avoid dropping or impact. The bone is durable but can be damaged by rough handling.

🔄

Maintenance

Periodically check for any signs of wear and contact us for professional cleaning or repair services.

Sustainability Impact

By choosing our fish bone jewelry, you're making a positive environmental impact:

  • Zero Waste: Every bone used in our jewelry would otherwise be discarded as processing waste
  • Sustainable Sourcing: All bones come from our own sustainable fishing operations
  • Eco-Friendly Processing: Our cleaning and treatment methods use environmentally safe products
  • Local Craftsmanship: Supporting local artisans and reducing transportation emissions
  • Unique Beauty: Each piece is one-of-a-kind, celebrating the natural diversity of fish bones
