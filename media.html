Explore news articles, videos, and media coverage featuring Two Rivers Fisheries and our role in Asian carp processing and export.

News Articles & Coverage

November 2017

Chinese Business Brings Prosperity to Fishermen on Mississippi

This article highlighted how Two Rivers Fisheries, founded by Chinese entrepreneur Angie Yu, provided local fishermen with a consistent market for their catch. The company's initiative aligned with U.S. efforts to control the Asian carp population in the Mississippi River.

Fishery Nation
Read Article
March 2018

Carp Conundrum: Too Many Fish, Not Enough Fishermen

This piece discussed the challenges faced by Two Rivers Fisheries in sourcing sufficient Asian carp due to a decline in commercial river fishing. Despite processing over two million pounds of Asian carp, the company emphasized the need for more full-time commercial fishermen.

Fishery Nation
Read Article
November 2018

Eating the Most Hated Fish on the Mississippi

An article explored the history of silver carp in the Mississippi River and the role of Two Rivers Fisheries in processing these fish. The company faced challenges in recruiting enough fishermen to meet processing demands.
