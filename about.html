About Two Rivers Fisheries

Learn about America's largest Asian carp processor and exporter, established in 2012 in Wickliffe, Kentucky.

Our Story

Two Rivers Fisheries was established in July 2012 in Wickliffe, Kentucky, with a mission to process and export Asian carp and other freshwater fish species. From our strategic location near the Mississippi and Ohio rivers, we have grown to become America's largest Asian carp processor and exporter.

Our company specializes in processing various species of carp including Yellow, Bighead, Silver, and Grass Carp, as well as buffalo and catfish. We contract professional fishermen to harvest fish from the Mississippi and Ohio rivers, Kentucky Lake, and Lake Barkley, ensuring a consistent supply of high-quality fish for processing.

Today, Two Rivers Fisheries stands as a leader in the Asian carp processing industry, serving global markets with safe, nutritious fish products while supporting local fishing communities and contributing to ecosystem management.
