Why Choose Two Rivers Fisheries?

Professional Harvesting

We contract professional fishermen to harvest Asian carp from the Mississippi and Ohio rivers, Kentucky Lake, and Lake Barkley.

🌍

Global Export

We process and export various species including Yellow, Bighead, Silver, and Grass Carp, as well as buffalo and catfish.
